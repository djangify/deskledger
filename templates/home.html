Welcome to Desk Ledger

Desk Ledger is a lightweight, bookkeeping tool for solo professionals running a one-person business. It has been made to be used on your computer but if you choose to you can self-host it. Either way, you own your data and it stays in your own environment.

About This Edition

Desk Ledger Local Edition is designed for users who prefer owning their own data and keeping it fully under their control. Using the Self-Hosted Edition means everything is processed directly on your own hosting server using a SQLite database.

This edition is ideal for solo professionals, privacy-focused users, and anyone who wants a straightforward bookkeeping tool without a subscription.
