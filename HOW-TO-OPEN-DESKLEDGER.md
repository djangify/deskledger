# How to Open DeskLedger

Follow these steps one at a time. It's easy!

## 1. Download it
Click the download link. You'll get a file called **DeskLedger.zip**.
Wait for it to finish downloading. It usually goes into your **Downloads** folder.

## 2. Unzip it
Find **DeskLedger.zip** in your Downloads folder.
Right-click it and choose **Extract All**, then click **Extract**.
This turns the zip into a normal folder called **DeskLedger**.

## 3. Open the app
Open the **DeskLedger** folder.
Find the file called **DeskLedger** with the gold logo, and **double-click** it.

## 4. If Windows shows a blue warning
The first time, Windows might say **"Windows protected your PC."**
Don't worry — this is normal for brand-new apps and DeskLedger is safe.

- Click **More info**
- Then click **Run anyway**

You only have to do this the first time.

## 5. The app opens
A window opens with the DeskLedger logo. The first time it may take a few seconds
to get ready. That's normal.

## 6. Log in
The first time you open DeskLedger, a small Notepad window opens by itself. It shows your
**email** and your **password** (a random one, made just for you).

- Type them into DeskLedger and click **Log in**.
- Then click **Settings**, then **Change password**, and choose a password of your own.
- When you have done that, **delete** the `first_login.txt` file. It is in the folder
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DeskLedger` (paste that into the File Explorer address bar).

**Forgot your password?** Close DeskLedger, then double-click
`DeskLedger-Reset-Password.bat` in the same folder as `DeskLedger.exe`. A new random
password opens in Notepad. Log in, change it, delete the file again. Your books are not
touched.

Keep your password somewhere safe, for example a password manager or a notebook that stays
at home. Your accounts are stored on this computer only, so nobody else can recover them for you.

## 7. Pin it so it's easy to find next time
While DeskLedger is open, look at the bar along the bottom of your screen.

- **Right-click** the DeskLedger icon down there
- Choose **Pin to taskbar**

To add it to your Start menu too:

- **Right-click** the **DeskLedger** app file
- Choose **Show more options** (if you see it), then **Pin to Start**

Now you can open DeskLedger any time with one click. Done!
